## 5. Hypothetical, Simulated, and Backtested Results

**HYPOTHETICAL AND SIMULATED PERFORMANCE RESULTS HAVE MANY INHERENT LIMITATIONS. NO REPRESENTATION IS BEING MADE THAT ANY ACCOUNT WILL OR IS LIKELY TO ACHIEVE PROFITS OR LOSSES SIMILAR TO THOSE SHOWN. IN FACT, THERE ARE FREQUENTLY SHARP DIFFERENCES BETWEEN HYPOTHETICAL PERFORMANCE RESULTS AND THE ACTUAL RESULTS SUBSEQUENTLY ACHIEVED BY ANY PARTICULAR TRADING PROGRAM.**

Much of the Service reports what a strategy would have done in the past or in a simulation. You should understand that:

- **Simulated capital is not real money.** The research environment uses a fictional starting balance of $100,000 ("Research Capital"). It is not an account, deposit, or property with any value, and results on it do not reflect how a real account of your size would perform; position sizes, liquidity, and market impact would differ.
- **Backtests are built with hindsight.** Strategies are designed, selected, and scored after the fact. Testing many variations and keeping those that look good creates "overfitting" and "selection bias," which can make results look far better than what will happen going forward. Our out-of-sample and walk-forward checks reduce, but do not eliminate, this risk.
- **Past performance does not indicate future results.** Markets change; a pattern that worked before may stop working without warning.
- **Assumptions may be unrealistic.** Simulations may not fully account for commissions, exchange and regulatory fees, bid/ask spreads, slippage, partial fills, borrowing costs for short positions, financing/margin interest, dividends, splits, delistings, taxes, halts, gaps, or order-execution delays. Real results are usually worse.
- **Data is imperfect.** Historical and live data may be delayed, incomplete, adjusted, revised, or wrong (see Section 8). A test that "passed" on flawed data proves nothing.
- **No one experienced the results shown.** Simulated results have no real-time decision-making, no emotional pressure, and no actual trades.
- Where a test ran on synthetic (generated) rather than real market data, it is labeled as such and is only a demonstration of the software.
